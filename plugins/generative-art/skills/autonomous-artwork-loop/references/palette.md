# Palette rules

High-saturation full-spectrum rainbows read as a "generative art demo" rather than an intentional piece. Decide the mood from the theme first, then the colors.

## Mood → palette

| Mood | Palette |
| --- | --- |
| Dark / moody | Black or near-black background, one or two muted accents |
| Warm / organic | Earthy desaturated tones, soft highlights |
| Cold / precise | Deep navy base, ice-blue or silver accents |
| Quiet / minimal | Near-monochrome with one restrained accent |
| Vibrant / neon | Dark background with a few glowing saturated hues, to read as emitted light |
| Graphic / bold | Black and white plus one strong color, sharp edges |

## Construction

- Three to five colors
- Dominant 60%, secondary 30%, accent 10%
- For most moods keep saturation moderate (around S ≤ 0.7 in HSV). Neon and graphic moods may use saturated accents
- A gradient spans 60-90° of hue, not the whole wheel
- Check contrast against the background before rendering
- Prefer curated palettes (from color systems or nature) over random RGB values

## Avoid

- An HSV sweep from 0° to 360° across one dimension
- Every element at full brightness
- Rainbow coloring as the fallback when no palette was chosen
- Large areas of pure high-saturation, high-luminance color; use gradients or additive glow to soften peaks
